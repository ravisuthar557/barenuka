About me

From Business Problems to Business Solutions

I am a Business Analyst focused on understanding business needs, translating them into clear requirements, and working with cross-functional teams to deliver practical solutions.

My experience includes end-to-end requirements gathering, BRD/FRD/PRD documentation, user stories, process modelling, wireframing, stakeholder communication, Agile/Scrum ceremonies, UAT coordination, risk identification, RFP/RFI management, market research and business case preparation.

I also bring a strong analytical mindset through Excel, Power BI, SQL and data-driven reporting, allowing me to connect business requirements with measurable insights.
